Privacy and limitations

Developer: Saleh Alayed
Updated: 17 November 2026

This notice describes HalalTag’s on-device text screening with English and Arabic controls, plus the separate webpages and public support channel linked below.

What screening does

HalalTag matches a small set of English ingredient terms and supported forms of E120 and E471 in text you type or paste. Screening runs on your device. It does not use a remote ingredient-analysis API, a camera, text recognition from images or barcode scanning.

The Arabic interface translates controls and explanations. It does not translate your ingredient text or expand the matching list to general Arabic ingredient names.

Your ingredient text and results

The current ingredient text and result are kept in memory for the current screen. The screening feature does not send them to the developer or a remote screening service, and it does not save a scan history or favorites list. A fresh app launch starts with empty ingredient text and no result.

Clear removes the current text and result from the app screen. Editing the text clears the previous result so you can screen the revised text again. Choosing a sample replaces the current text and screens the sample.

Screening does not require an account. This feature includes no advertising or analytics SDK and does not request camera, photo library, microphone or location permissions.

Your language setting

The app saves your English or Arabic interface choice in its local settings and reuses it on later launches. Changing the interface language keeps your current ingredient text and result. Clear does not reset the language choice.

To change it, open Privacy and limitations in the app, choose the globe menu labeled App language, then select English or العربية. The language links on these webpages change only the page you are reading.

Your device and keyboard

Keyboard, dictation, clipboard, device backups and other iOS services are governed by your device settings and their providers’ practices. Clearing this app’s text does not erase your clipboard. This notice about HalalTag’s screening feature does not guarantee the handling of data by those separate services or the security of your device.

Visiting these webpages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Your browser sends a request to GitHub when you visit. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including visits made without signing in. This is separate from on-device screening. These pages contain no advertising, analytics script, embedded external media or contact form.

Read GitHub Pages data collection information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Public support requests

You can ask a non-sensitive app or privacy question through the HalalTag public issue channel. Posting requires a GitHub account. GitHub handles your username, request text and any attachments you submit. They are visible to the repository maintainer and other people on the internet.

Support requests are public. Do not post private contact details, credentials, personal documents or sensitive ingredient examples. Use a short, non-sensitive example to explain a problem.

Issue content may remain in the public repository and may be copied by others. Use GitHub’s available editing and privacy controls. If you have a question about an existing post, refer to its issue number without repeating private information. This channel provides no response-time, removal or confidentiality guarantee. GitHub’s privacy statement explains its own privacy-request options.

What results mean

A match identifies supported words in the supplied text. It does not establish ingredient presence, source, processing, cross-contamination or halal certification. Unrecognized terms, spellings and languages may be missed. Simple label claims may need context review; the app cannot verify them.

No supported match means the status remains unknown. It does not establish that a product is halal or certified. Check the original label and ask the manufacturer or a qualified certifier when source or certification is unclear.

Questions about this notice

Read the support instructions before opening a public request. For questions about GitHub’s handling of your account or website data, use the privacy options in the GitHub Privacy Statement.
