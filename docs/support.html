Support

Updated: 17 November 2026

HalalTag helps you review a small supported set of English ingredient terms and forms of E120 and E471. Its controls and explanations are available in English and Arabic.

Check ingredient text

  1. Type or paste the English ingredient text you want to review.
  2. Choose Screen text.
  3. Read the result and the supported words that matched.
  4. Check the original label. Ask the manufacturer or a qualified certifier when ingredient source or certification is unclear.

You can also choose one of the included English sample texts. A sample replaces the current text and is screened immediately. Editing any text clears the previous result so you can screen the revised text again.

Choose English or Arabic

  1. Open Privacy and limitations in the app.
  2. Open the globe menu labeled App language.
  3. Choose English or العربية, then choose Done to return.

Your choice is saved for later launches. Switching languages keeps your current text and result. The webpage language links change only these pages.

Arabic controls do not mean Arabic ingredient screening. The interface does not translate your input or extend the limited English matching list. Other languages, ingredients, spellings and synonyms can be missed.

Remove the current text and result

Choose Clear. It clears the app’s current ingredient text and result, but does not reset the language setting or erase your clipboard. There is no saved scan history or favorites list. A fresh app launch starts with empty ingredient text and no result.

Understand a result

A matched term may need ingredient-source or label-context checks. If no supported term is found, the result remains unknown. A phrase such as “alcohol-free” may require context review; the app cannot verify that claim.

HalalTag does not determine halal status or issue certification. Word matches cannot verify ingredient presence, origin, processing or cross-contamination. Check the original label and seek reliable sourcing or certification information when needed.

Camera, barcode and online analysis

The screening feature accepts typed or pasted text. It does not scan photos or barcodes and does not use a camera or a remote ingredient-analysis API. You do not need an account to screen text.

Ask a question or report a problem

Use the HalalTag public issue channel for non-sensitive questions, bug reports or suggestions. GitHub requires sign-in to post. You can also read existing requests.

This is a public channel. Other people can read your GitHub username, request and attachments. Do not post private contact details, credentials, personal documents or sensitive information. Use a non-sensitive example instead of a personal ingredient list or screenshot.

  1. Give the issue a short title that describes the problem.
  2. Include the app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and selected app language.
  3. Describe your steps, what happened and what you expected. Include only the minimum non-sensitive example needed to explain the issue.

This channel provides no guaranteed response time. It is not private messaging and cannot verify a product’s ingredient sourcing or halal certification.
